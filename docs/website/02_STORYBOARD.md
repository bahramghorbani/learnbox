# Website Storyboard

The website is a single continuous journey. Each scene must have a clear entry, action, message, and exit. Avoid building unrelated sections that merely sit below one another.

## Scene 01 — Enter the LearnBox world

### Goal

Immediately communicate the brand, product, and emotional tone.

### Visual

A cinematic purple environment with soft depth, floating vocabulary cards, subtle light particles, and BuBu as the central guide.

### Copy direction

Headline:
«کلمه‌ها را فقط حفظ نکن؛ برای همیشه یاد بگیر.»

Supporting copy:
LearnBox با مرور هوشمند، جعبه لایتنر و همراهی BuBu، یادگیری زبان آلمانی را به یک عادت لذت‌بخش تبدیل می‌کند.

### Actions

- Android download
- Open web app
- Watch how it works

### Motion

BuBu reacts gently to the page entrance. Cards float with restrained depth. The camera advances into the learning world as the user scrolls.

---

## Scene 02 — The forgetting problem

### Goal

Make the user recognize the core pain point.

### Visual

BuBu is surrounded by scattered cards, notes, and forgotten words. The environment feels disorganized but not stressful.

### Copy direction

«کلمه‌های زیادی یاد می‌گیری، اما چند روز بعد فراموششان می‌کنی؟»

### Motion

Cards drift away or fade until LearnBox begins organizing them.

---

## Scene 03 — The Leitner system appears

### Goal

Explain the logic of structured review visually.

### Visual

Cards enter a clear sequence of boxes or stages. Hard cards return sooner. Learned cards advance.

### Feature messages

- مرور فاصله‌دار
- تکرار هوشمند
- تمرکز روی واژه‌های دشوار
- برنامه مرور روزانه
- کاهش فراموشی

### Motion

Scroll-linked card paths. Avoid excessive bouncing. Movement should communicate progression.

---

## Scene 04 — Learning beyond plain text

### Goal

Show that vocabulary learning includes context and media.

### Visual

A vocabulary card expands into pronunciation, Persian meaning, example, image, grammatical gender, plural form, and level.

### Possible feature set

Only show features approved and available in the app.

Potential visual items:

- تلفظ صوتی
- معنی فارسی
- مثال کاربردی
- تصویر
- جنسیت اسم
- شکل جمع
- سطح واژه
- تمرین شنیداری

### Motion

The card transforms rather than being replaced by unrelated UI.

---

## Scene 05 — Learning paths

### Goal

Show that LearnBox serves different user goals.

### Visual

BuBu reaches a stylized learning map.

### Paths

- مهاجرت کاری
- مهاجرت تحصیلی
- مکالمه روزمره
- زبان عمومی
- آمادگی آزمون

Work and academic migration should receive the strongest visual priority.

### Interaction

Hover, tap, or scroll focus changes the path environment and sample cards.

---

## Scene 06 — Gamification and daily habit

### Goal

Present consistency as rewarding and achievable.

### Visual

BuBu interacts with streaks, goals, badges, XP, daily missions, and weekly progress.

DuDu may appear here only if approved as the energetic secondary companion.

### Feature direction

- زنجیره روزانه
- امتیاز و سطح
- مأموریت‌های روزانه
- نشان‌ها
- هدف هفتگی
- یادآوری مرور
- واکنش‌های BuBu

### Motion

Progress elements animate forward. Avoid casino-like or manipulative visual language.

---

## Scene 07 — Real product experience

### Goal

Ground the story in the actual application.

### Visual

Real LearnBox screens inside device frames or clean floating panels.

### Screens

- home,
- today’s review,
- vocabulary card,
- practice result,
- progress analytics,
- profile or level.

### Rule

Replace all placeholder screens with validated product screens before production release.

---

## Scene 08 — Choose how to start

### Goal

Provide clear platform-specific access.

### Android

- Café Bazaar button
- QR code
- release status

### iPhone, iPad, and browser

- web app button
- QR code
- optional “Add to Home Screen” guide

### Copy rule

Use “نسخه وب برای iPhone و iPad” rather than implying native App Store distribution.

---

## Scene 09 — LearnBox community

### Goal

Connect the website with official channels.

### Channels

- Telegram: updates and announcements
- Instagram: short lessons and visual content
- LinkedIn: product and business updates
- Pinterest: educational visuals and flashcards

### Visual

Do not reduce this section to four tiny footer icons. Give each channel a purpose.

---

## Scene 10 — Final invitation

### Goal

End the story with a clear emotional and functional action.

### Visual

BuBu reaches a completed LearnBox environment. The cards are organized, progress is visible, and the world feels calm.

### Copy direction

«اولین کلمه، شروع یک مسیر تازه است.»

### Primary action

«یادگیری را با BuBu شروع کن»

### Exit

Footer with official links, legal pages, contact, social channels, and current release information.
